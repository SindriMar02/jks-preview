Villa Almendro 67

A modern villa with a heated private pool, a rooftop solarium and a game room, a hundred metres from the Crystal Lagoon.

↓

At a glance

Space for everyone, sun on every side

Villa Almendro 67 is a modern villa on the Costa Cálida, built for families, groups of friends and golf holidays. Generous rooms inside, and outside a pool, a covered terrace and a rooftop solarium with a view over the resort and the lagoon.

Guests have the whole villa to themselves: the pool, the rooftop, the terraces, the game room and private parking. Arrival is self check-in with a lockbox.
